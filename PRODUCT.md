# Blog Imóveis Campos — Product Context

## Identity
**Name:** Blog Imóveis Campos  
**Tagline:** Posts diários, opinativos, sobre mercado imobiliário local  
**Region:** Campos dos Goytacazes, RJ  
**Register:** Product (app/blog UI, not brand marketing)

## Users
- **Primary:** Property seekers (compradores/alugadores residencial)
- **Secondary:** Real estate professionals, investors
- **Tertiary:** Curious locals interested in local market trends

## Brand Voice
- **Tone:** Opinativo, direto, factual
- **Personality:** Analista de mercado experiente, confiável, sem medo de ter opinião
- **Aesthetic:** Modern, clean, data-driven, journalistic
- **Anti-references:** Clickbait, fake urgency, empty hype, generic real estate jargon

## Strategic Principles
1. **Credibility first** — E-E-A-T compliance for AdSense (expertise, experience, authority, trustworthiness)
2. **Content quality gates** — 70% quality threshold before publish
3. **Mobile-first** — 70%+ users on mobile (Brazil market)
4. **Performance** — Fast load, low bounce rate (impacts RPM)
5. **SEO-friendly** — Long-tail keywords, internal linking, schema.org

## Key Features
- Homepage: post list (latest first, infinite scroll ready)
- Post detail: full article + related posts + newsletter CTA
- Newsletter signup: email collection for engagement signal
- AdSense placements: display + in-feed (non-intrusive)
- Tags/categories: explore by neighborhood/property type

## Success Metrics
- RPM ≥ R$8/1k views
- Bounce rate <60%
- 2+ min avg time on page
- 1k+ pageviews/month by month 3
- 5-10 posts/day sustained

## Constraints
- **Legal:** All content sourced (Zap, VivaReal, public data)
- **Content:** No investment advice without disclaimer
- **Compliance:** YMYL (Your Money Your Life) category — must earn AdSense trust
- **Tech:** Next.js 14, Vercel, Supabase, Claude API (cost-optimized)
